## [0.1.0]

### Added

- Playable Artoria, Gilgamesh, EMIYA and Altera, with progression deeds and true-name awakening.
- Gilgamesh's five-section King's Vault with searching, sorting and configurable volleys.
- Gate of Babylon's area, single-target and aimed volleys, with returning treasures and optional disposable copies.
- Bab-ilu's drawing and storage of Ea, Ea's compressed air and the held Enuma Elish beam.
- Enkidu's binding chains and tightening attacks, strengthened by the target's divinity.
- EMIYA's Rho Aias, with rank-dependent petals, adjustable placement and projectile resistance.
- Kanshou and Bakuya's paired melee attacks and thrown swords.
- Sword tracing, limited-use projections and Trace On's projected volleys.
- EMIYA's sword-firing bow, triple shots, Caladbolg blasts and faster Archer bow handling.
- Altera's Natural Body passives and Crest of the Star's speed and critical attacks.
- Photon Ray's charged sword dash, frontal cone shield and finishing knockback.
- Teardrop Photon Ray's locked sky circles, red Condensed beam and single wide Spread beam with one merged ground blast.
- Artoria's starting Caliburn, Mana Burst, Mana Shot, Mana Slash, Strike Air and Excalibur.
- Avalon, found rarely in chests: held, it blocks all damage; carried, it regenerates its holder while Artoria is online.
- Animated weapon models, including Hrunting, Excalibur and Avalon.
- Protected signature weapons, configurable balance and optional weapon block destruction.
- EMIYA's Sword Body skill, with thorns, damage reduction and regeneration.
- Kanshou and Bakuya throw animation.
- Unlimited Blade Works ground swords match EMIYA's traced arsenal, with per-player amount, spread and tilt settings.
- Enuma Elish shatters reality marbles.
- Divine constructs can't be traced, except Excalibur, which costs 99% mana and drains health.
- Mobs that are servants can use Trace On and Unlimited Blade Works.
- An Ea without an owner belongs to the first player who holds it.
